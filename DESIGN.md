# Atmar POS Design Direction

Status: draft direction for the dummy-data frontend prototype. Replace this document when the product owner supplies a formal brand guide.

## Identity

Atmar POS is an operational tool for local coffee shops. It should feel warm enough to belong in a cafe, but direct enough for staff working under time pressure.

## Personality

Grounded, quick, legible, and calm under pressure. Avoid luxury-cafe decoration and generic technology styling.

## Palette

- Ink: deep brown-black for primary text and operational structure.
- Paper: warm cream for the main surface.
- Clay: burnt orange used only for primary actions and active focus.
- Sage: green reserved for completed and ready states.
- Amber and red are reserved for attention and failure states.

## Typography

Geist is retained for the prototype because its compact proportions keep dense operational screens readable. A future brand pass should reassess the typeface.

## Mood

Warm counter service, printed order slips, and clear station labels. Surfaces stay mostly flat so status and actions carry the hierarchy.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Decision reasons

- Warm cream reduces the sterile dashboard feeling and reflects the coffee-shop context.
- Clay marks the main action so it is easy to find without coloring every control.
- A persistent navigation rail matches the PRD's multi-module staff workflow.
- Mobile uses a compact top bar and menu because the desktop navigation cannot be squeezed onto a phone.
- Cards are used for work objects such as orders, tables, and tickets. Reports and configuration use rows and tables instead.
- Motion is limited to view transitions and state changes so it guides attention without distracting staff.
